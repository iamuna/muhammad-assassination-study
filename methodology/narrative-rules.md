# Narrative Rules

The narrative book is not allowed to outrun the evidence ledger.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. No invented internal monologue for Prophet Muhammad ﷺ or any other historical actor.
2. No invented dialogue presented as historical dialogue.
3. No invented motives when motive is not supported.
4. No invented weather, time-of-day, gestures, room arrangements, crowd reactions, or scenery merely for cinematic effect.
5. Miraculous material is reported according to the source and labeled through source handling; it is neither silently removed nor embellished.
6. Conflicting reports are not secretly harmonized.
7. Exact quotations require separate quotation-level assessment.
8. Weak but important material may enter only under its Narrative Permission level.
9. Battles, persecution, direct assault, assassination conspiracy, attempted poisoning, and wartime attempts to kill a commander remain analytically distinct.
10. If evidence ends, the story stops.

## Literary freedom allowed

The prose may use:
- pacing;
- chapter structure;
- neutral connective sentences;
- supported geographical/contextual description;
- carefully signaled uncertainty;
- transitions that do not add historical propositions.

## Audit rule

Every finished narrative chapter must be traceable to approved Claim IDs before it is considered final.
