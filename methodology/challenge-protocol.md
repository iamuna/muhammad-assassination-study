# Adversarial Challenge Protocol

Before a claim can move from provisional to accepted, the project must attempt to disconfirm it.

## Minimum challenge pass

For each material claim:
1. Identify the strongest contrary or alternative evidence.
2. Test whether corroborating sources are truly independent.
3. Search for earlier forms lacking later dramatic details.
4. Identify variant wording that changes intention, identity, chronology, or causation.
5. Test whether translation choices inflate certainty.
6. Test whether later theological, sectarian, political, legal, or literary interests could have shaped the report.
7. Test whether the event is being misclassified as assassination rather than assault, battle, persecution, or threat.
8. Record unresolved objections even when the claim remains accepted.

## Red-team result

Every reviewed claim receives one:
- SURVIVED — objections considered; grade retained.
- DOWNGRADED — challenge materially reduced confidence.
- SPLIT — strong core separated from weaker details.
- RECLASSIFIED — event category changed.
- REJECTED — claim cannot responsibly support narrative history.
- OPEN — evidence remains genuinely inconclusive.

No claim becomes stronger merely because objections were not searched for.
