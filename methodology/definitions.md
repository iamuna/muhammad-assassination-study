# Operational Definitions

These definitions exist to prevent inflated counting.

## Assassination conspiracy
A deliberate plan directed specifically at killing Muhammad ﷺ as an individual, normally outside ordinary immediate battlefield engagement.

## Attempted assassination
A conspirator or attacker progresses beyond mere discussion/threat into an overt act, approach, deployment, poisoning, armed positioning, or other execution step directed toward killing him.

## Direct potentially lethal assault
Violence capable of causing death, but surviving evidence does not establish premeditated intent to kill.

## Threat
An expressed intention or promise of violence without sufficient evidence that execution began.

## Battlefield attack
An attempt to kill Muhammad while he is functioning as a combatant or commander in open battle. Historically important but not automatically counted as assassination.

## Persecution / mob violence
Collective or public violence not sufficiently individualized as a deliberate assassination operation.

## Duplicate
A later retelling or differently named version of the same underlying event.

## Misclassified
A real or reported event that does not satisfy the project's operational assassination definition.

These categories are provisional research tools. Borderline cases must preserve the uncertainty rather than forcing a binary label.
