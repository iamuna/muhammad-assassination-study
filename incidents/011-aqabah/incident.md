# INC-011 — ʿAqabah mountain-pass conspiracy after Tabūk

## Provisional conclusion
This incident has a strong core in Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2779 and a more explicit attempt narrative in Musnad Aḥmad 23792 and parallel witnesses. The group is described as men of the “ʿAqabah,” with twelve singled out as hostile to God and His Messenger. In Aḥmad's version, masked riders crowd the Prophet's mount on a narrow pass; the stated intention was to cause the mount to bolt so that Muhammad would be thrown.

## Classification
- Conspiracy by members of the Tabūk return party: **H1/H2**
- Overt attempt to throw Muhammad from the pass: **H2**
- Exact number actively culpable: **H2/H3** (fourteen/fifteen total, three excused, twelve condemned in the reports)
- Individual identities: not admitted without stronger source control.

## Primary anchors
- Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2779
- Musnad Aḥmad 23792 and parallels
