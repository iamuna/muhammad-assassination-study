# INC-004 — ʿUmayr ibn Wahb / Ṣafwān plot

## Provisional conclusion
Ibn Isḥāq, as preserved by Ibn Hishām, reports that after Badr ʿUmayr b. Wahb told Ṣafwān b. Umayyah that, but for his debt and dependents, he would ride to Medina and kill Muhammad. Ṣafwān agreed to cover the debt and family. The report continues that ʿUmayr had his sword sharpened and poisoned and traveled to Medina.

The upstream attribution is Ibn Isḥāq → Muḥammad b. Jaʿfar b. al-Zubayr → ʿUrwa b. al-Zubayr, making this an early sīrah trajectory but not a ṣaḥīḥayn-level report.

## Classification
- Assassination conspiracy: **H3/H4**
- Overt attempt: **H4** (travel with weapon under the reported plan)
- Poisoned sword detail: **H4/H5**
- Exact private dialogue: **H5**

## Narrative permission
- Basic reported plot: NP-2/3
- Exact speeches and poisoned-blade detail: NP-3

## Primary anchor
Ibn Hishām preserving Ibn Isḥāq, “Islam of ʿUmayr b. Wahb.”
