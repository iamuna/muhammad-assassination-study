# INC-006 — Sword-under-the-tree incident

## Provisional conclusion
This is one of the strongest non-Khaybar attempt reports. Jābir b. ʿAbd Allāh's account is preserved through multiple canonical routes in al-Bukhārī and Muslim. A polytheist found Muhammad resting with his sword hanging from a tree, drew the sword, stood over him, and demanded who would protect him.

The man's name is supplied in one Bukhārī route as **Ghawrath b. al-Ḥārith**.

## Classification
- Overt attempted killing / armed coercion: **H1/H2**
- Assassination-like surprise attack: **H1/H2**
- Name Ghawrath: **H2**
- Later embellishments in which Gabriel physically strikes the attacker: separately graded, not part of the strongest canonical core.

## Primary anchors
- Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 2910 / 4136
- Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 843a / 843c

## Narrative permission
NP-1 for the sword seizure and threat.
