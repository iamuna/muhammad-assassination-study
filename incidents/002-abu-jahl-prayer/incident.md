# INC-002 — Abū Jahl at prayer

## Provisional conclusion
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2797 preserves an explicit threat and an overt execution step. Abū Jahl swore that if he saw Muhammad prostrating, he would tread on his neck or grind his face into the dust. The report then says he **came to Muhammad while he was praying intending to tread on his neck**, approached, and suddenly retreated.

## Classification
- Assassination / lethal assault intent: **H1 core**
- Overt attempt: **H1/H2**
- Narrative permission: NP-1 for threat and approach; theological explanation attributed as source content.

## Claim grades
- Abū Jahl explicitly threatened to tread on Muhammad's neck while he prayed: **H1**
- He approached Muhammad during prayer intending to carry out that threat: **H1**
- He retreated before physical contact: **H1**
- The report's fire/terror/wings and angelic explanation: preserve as transmitted theological content, not naturalistic historical mechanism.

## Primary anchor
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2797.
