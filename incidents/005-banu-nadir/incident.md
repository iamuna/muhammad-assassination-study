# INC-005 — Banū al-Naḍīr stone report

## Provisional conclusion
Ibn Isḥāq/Ibn Hishām and al-Wāqidī preserve a narrative in which Muhammad visited Banū al-Naḍīr regarding blood-money obligations, sat beside a wall, and members of the group proposed dropping a large stone on him from above. ʿAmr b. Jiḥāsh is named as the volunteer.

The story is important in the traditional explanation of the rupture with Banū al-Naḍīr, but the assassination mechanism itself is not anchored in a canonical hadith report of comparable strength to Khaybar or Ghawrath.

## Classification
- Assassination conspiracy: **H3/H4**
- Overt attempt (man ascends to drop stone): **H4**
- Named perpetrator ʿAmr b. Jiḥāsh: **H4**
- Exact divine-warning mechanism: attributed tradition

## Caution
The story's political function in explaining the expulsion makes source-dependency and narrative shaping especially important. Qurʾān 59 supplies historical context for Banū al-Naḍīr but does not itself narrate the stone plot.

## Narrative permission
NP-3 for the assassination mechanism until exact transmission audit is complete.
