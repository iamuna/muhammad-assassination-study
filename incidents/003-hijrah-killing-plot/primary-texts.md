# INC-003 — Primary Evidence Register

## P-003-01 — Qurʾān 8:30
Source ID: SRC-QUR-001

Arabic:
`وَإِذْ يَمْكُرُ بِكَ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا لِيُثْبِتُوكَ أَوْ يَقْتُلُوكَ أَوْ يُخْرِجُوكَ وَيَمْكُرُونَ وَيَمْكُرُ اللَّهُ وَاللَّهُ خَيْرُ الْمَاكِرِينَ`

Evidentiary function:
Direct textual evidence for a hostile plot involving restraint/detention, killing, or expulsion.

What it does **not** establish by itself:
- Dār al-Nadwah;
- Abū Jahl;
- a multi-clan execution team;
- ʿAlī in the bed;
- guards around the house;
- cave pursuit;
- spider web.

## P-003-02 — al-Ṭabarī, tafsīr of Q 8:30
Source ID: SRC-TAF-TAB-E01

Reports 15956–15962 preserve early glosses of `ليثبتوك` as binding, restraining, or imprisoning.

Representative trajectories include:
- Ibn ʿAbbās;
- Mujāhid;
- Qatādah;
- al-Suddī;
- ʿAṭāʾ;
- Ibn Zayd.

Function:
Confirms the early interpretation of the verse's first option without importing the later full council narrative.

## P-003-03 — al-Ṭabarī report 15965 / Ibn Isḥāq material
Attributed route includes:
Ibn Isḥāq → ʿAbd Allāh b. Abī Najīḥ → Mujāhid → Ibn ʿAbbās,
along with another Ibn ʿAbbās route quoted in the report.

Function:
Dār al-Nadwah; Najdī elder; competing proposals; Abū Jahl's joint-clan proposal; blood-liability rationale; warning before departure.

Caution:
The long narrative must be decomposed. Its detailed speeches cannot inherit the confidence of Q 8:30 merely because the verse is quoted at the end.

## P-003-04 — Ibn Hishām preserving Ibn Isḥāq
Source ID: SRC-SIR-HIS-E01

The Ibn Hishām recension preserves the same broad Dār al-Nadwah narrative, including Abū Jahl's proposal that one young man from each clan participate so responsibility would be distributed.

Function:
Primary surviving sīrah witness to the famous council narrative.

Dependency:
Not independent of Ibn Isḥāq material quoted by al-Ṭabarī merely because it survives in another book.

## P-003-05 — ʿAbd al-Razzāq, al-Muṣannaf 9743
Source ID: SRC-HAD-ARZ-E01

Route:
ʿAbd al-Razzāq → Maʿmar → ʿUthmān al-Jazarī → Miqsam → Ibn ʿAbbās.

Core:
- Quraysh deliberates in Mecca;
- restrain / kill / expel;
- ʿAlī occupies Muhammad's sleeping place;
- watchers believe he is Muhammad;
- Muhammad reaches the cave;
- pursuit follows;
- spider-web detail.

Critical judgment:
This same trajectory appears as Aḥmad 3251. Shuʿayb al-Arnaʾūṭ grades the isnād weak because of ʿUthmān al-Jazarī.

## P-003-06 — Qatādah material preserved by ʿAbd al-Razzāq
Source ID: SRC-HAD-ARZ-E01

Maʿmar reports Qatādah's Dār al-Nadwah narrative:
- Najdī elder;
- expulsion proposal;
- confinement proposal;
- joint killing proposal;
- Muhammad and Abū Bakr to Thawr;
- ʿAlī in the sleeping place;
- watchers mistake him for Muhammad.

Function:
A separate transmission trajectory for important elements of the execution-stage story.

Caution:
Its precise dependence on other early maghāzī traditions is not yet established.

## P-003-07 — ʿUrwa-related Hijrah narrative
Source ID: SRC-MAG-URW-E01

Preserved through later witnesses, this narrative connects Quraysh's pre-Hijrah decision against Muhammad with:
- imminent departure;
- ʿAlī masking the departure by occupying the sleeping place;
- pursuit/search;
- refuge in the cave.

Function:
Early-framework corroboration for the link between the plot and the Hijrah.

Caution:
The ʿUrwa corpus is reconstructed from later witnesses; every detail requires witness-level provenance.

## P-003-08 — Aḥmad 3251
Route:
ʿAbd al-Razzāq → Maʿmar → ʿUthmān al-Jazarī → Miqsam → Ibn ʿAbbās.

Edition note:
In the Arnaʾūṭ edition this report is at vol. 5, p. 301.

Modern editorial judgment:
**isnād weak**, with the criticism centered on ʿUthmān al-Jazarī.

Historical use:
May corroborate the existence of a circulating early narrative, but its weaker details cannot be promoted to NP-1 solely from this chain.

## P-003-09 — variant with Abū Ṭālib
Some tafsīr material preserves a version in which Abū Ṭālib asks Muhammad what Quraysh intends.

Chronological problem:
Abū Ṭālib's death is traditionally placed years before the Hijrah.

Function:
Important negative evidence showing that the transmission complex contains variant and chronologically problematic expansions.

## Publication rule
Final source-book citations will use the locked edition, volume/page/report number for each witness.
