# INC-003 — Variants and Tensions

## 1. What does `ليثبتوك` mean?

Early tafsīr preserves:
- bind/restrain;
- imprison;
- in one problematic variant, a different reading/interpretation.

Project position:
The stable semantic core is **physical restraint/detention**. The source book should preserve the lexical variants instead of forcing one English word everywhere.

---

## 2. Was there a formal Dār al-Nadwah meeting?

The Qurʾān does not name the meeting place.

Ibn Isḥāq/Ibn Hishām and Qatādah material place the deliberation at Dār al-Nadwah.

Project position:
Historically plausible and multiply transmitted at the narrative level, but below the confidence of the Qurʾānic core.

---

## 3. Who proposed the killing plan?

The famous sīrah form attributes the decisive plan to Abū Jahl.

Q 8:30 gives no proposer.

Project position:
Abū Jahl attribution is a separate claim. Do not write it as if the verse named him.

---

## 4. What exactly was the plan?

Ibn Isḥāq's form:
representatives from multiple clans would act together so the liability could not be concentrated on one clan.

Qatādah material preserves a closely related multi-tribal concept.

Project position:
The multi-clan mechanism is stronger than the exact speech attributed to Abū Jahl, but weaker than the bare existence of a killing plot.

---

## 5. Najdī elder / Iblīs

The Najdī elder appears in the expanded council narratives.

Historical problem:
The claim is theological/supernatural in form and is not required to establish the human conspiracy.

Project position:
Preserve as part of the transmitted tradition with attribution; do not use it as a factual historical mechanism in NP-1 prose.

---

## 6. ʿAlī in Muhammad's sleeping place

The Miqsam/Ibn ʿAbbās route and other traditions preserve ʿAlī occupying Muhammad's place.

The route through ʿUthmān al-Jazarī is weak in Arnaʾūṭ's grading, while other material offers partial support.

Project position:
Probable enough to retain provisionally, but not at H1.

---

## 7. Were attackers already stationed outside?

Some narrative forms say hostile watchers remained around Muhammad's sleeping place, expecting him to be there.

This is the crucial classification boundary:
- council decision = conspiracy;
- armed/hostile positioning for execution = attempt.

Project position:
The execution-stage claim is provisionally H3, not H1.

---

## 8. Cave spider web

The spider-web detail occurs in the weak Miqsam route and later famous retellings.

Several modern hadith critics reject that specific report as weak.

Project position:
It is not necessary for the Hijrah core and receives low historical confidence.

---

## 9. Abū Ṭālib variant

One exegetical report includes Abū Ṭālib in a conversation connected to Q 8:30.

Chronological tension:
the standard chronology places his death well before the Hijrah.

Project position:
This is evidence of variant narrative development and should not be harmonized into the pre-Hijrah council account.

---

## 10. Exact dialogue

The polished speeches in the long council scene are far more precise than the evidence needed to establish the underlying event.

Project position:
Treat as literary/transmitted wording, not verbatim minutes of the council.
