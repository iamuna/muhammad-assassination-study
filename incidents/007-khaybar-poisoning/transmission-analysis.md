## Independence findings

### Do not double count
- Bukhārī 2617 and Muslim 2190a share the same Anas/Hishām/Shuʿbah trunk.
- Bukhārī 3169, 4249 and 5777 are not three independent historical witnesses; they belong to the same Abū Hurayrah/Saʿīd/al-Layth family.
- Aḥmad and Ibn Saʿd forms through ʿIkrimah/Hilāl/ʿAbbād should be treated as one upstream family unless a divergence below that trunk becomes historically relevant.

### Genuine value of convergence
Even after collapsing dependencies, the poisoning core is preserved through materially different upstream routes:
- Anas;
- Abū Hurayrah via Saʿīd al-Maqburī;
- Ibn ʿAbbās via ʿIkrimah;
- additional weaker/disputed Jābir and Abū Salamah material;
- sīrah/maghāzī traditions.

This is why the core receives a much stronger assessment than details known only from one narrative expansion.
