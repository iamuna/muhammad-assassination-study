## Counting rule

The project will **not publish one inflated total** by treating every battle, assault, persecution, weak report, and duplicate as a separate assassination attempt.

Instead it will distinguish:
- secure assassination/attempt cores;
- probable/attributed attempts;
- direct lethal assaults;
- battlefield cases;
- rejected/duplicate candidates.

This register remains open to new evidence.
