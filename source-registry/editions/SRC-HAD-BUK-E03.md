Its value is different from a modern critical edition:
- textual-control comparison against later editions;
- checking chapter headings, wording, and historical pagination;
- identifying whether later printed variants arise before or after modern editorial intervention.

Because only volume 9 is present, it cannot serve as a complete Bukhārī edition for the project.
