## Critical correction
For the preferred Abū Dāwūd E01 (Arnaʾūṭ/Qarah Ballī 2009), hadith 4510–4512 are in **volume 6**, because the online set places hadith 3855–4595 there. “Volume 4” applies to the older Muḥyī al-Dīn ʿAbd al-Ḥamīd edition, not E01.
