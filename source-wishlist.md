# Source Wishlist

This file will be populated with exact editions after the first source audit.

Priority codes:
- **P0** — essential
- **P1** — important
- **P2** — useful corroboration/context
- **P3** — specialist/disputed

## Initial categories

### P0
- Qur'an — Arabic text and a stable scholarly reference edition
- Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī
- Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim
- Musnad Aḥmad
- Sunan Abī Dāwūd
- Ibn Hishām, al-Sīrah al-Nabawiyyah
- al-Wāqidī, Kitāb al-Maghāzī
- Ibn Saʿd, al-Ṭabaqāt al-Kubrā
- al-Balādhurī, relevant works
- al-Ṭabarī, Tārīkh al-rusul wa-l-mulūk
- al-Bayhaqī, Dalāʾil al-Nubuwwah

### P1
- Jāmiʿ al-Tirmidhī
- Sunan al-Nasāʾī
- Sunan Ibn Mājah
- Muwaṭṭaʾ Mālik
- early tafsīr corpora relevant to cited verses
- rijāl / narrator criticism works required for disputed chains

### P1 Modern scholarship
Exact editions/titles will be selected deliberately, especially for:
- early sīrah formation;
- hadith historical method;
- isnād-cum-matn analysis;
- Qur'anic historical context;
- chronology of Muhammad's life;
- seventh-century western Arabian social and political context.

Do not acquire random editions merely to fill the list. Exact edition requests will be added as research requires them.
